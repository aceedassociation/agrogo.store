# Images — version 7

The 90 supplementary product families now use real product photographs captured from their source catalogue product pages. No AI-generated picture or family drawing is used for these 90 families. Product cards, detail pages, home categories and category headers use the replacement photographs.

Ordinary cropping, resizing and white backgrounds prepare the photographs for the shop. External advertising banners are cropped out and supplier names are obscured where visible, preserving the photographed objects. Solar kits can use source photographs assembled into a component montage. The photograph represents the family; the selected size or model may differ.

All images are stored locally in assets/photographs. The original 252 product families and their images are unchanged. Some existing catalogue families still carry their previous illustrative-image label. Source URLs and processing records remain in the private work folder rather than the website package.
